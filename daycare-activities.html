Ynmo · Mobile

Daycare activities

Parents couldn’t find their children’s photos. I redesigned how teachers record a child’s day and how parents see it, in the teacher and family apps at once.

Role

Product Designer, the only designer

Team

Me, 1 PM, 1 mobile developer

Timeline

About 1 month

Status

Shipped in both apps

Context

In Ynmo’s apps, daycare teachers share a child’s day, like photos, meals, naps and health, and parents follow it in the family app. But parents couldn’t find their children’s photos. Past activities were buried deep in the app.

The goal: help parents find their child’s day, and make it quick for teachers to share it.

Key decisions

01 · Family app

All activities in one place

All past activities, with a photo or without, now sit in one Activities tab in the child’s profile, filterable by date.

Why: they used to be buried deep in the app.

02 · Family app

The latest on the home screen

The business asked for a social feed. The home screen now lists the child’s latest activities, and parents can load more or refresh to see new ones. Parents and teachers can also react and comment.

Why: it’s the first screen parents see, so they don’t have to go looking for new activities.

03 · Teacher app

One button for all activities

Instead of 8 activity tiles that each opened their own form, one button opens the form and the teacher picks the type.

Why: teachers add every activity the same way, from one place.

04 · Teacher app

Only what each activity needs

Each of the 8 types (general, health, mood, meal, nap, medications, diaper, supplies) asks only what’s relevant to it.

Why: teachers fill in less, and parents see the details that matter for that activity, like how long a nap was.

05 · Both apps

Health updates parents can’t miss

Health activities record the child’s condition and temperature, and an activity can be marked urgent for the family.

Why: parents need to notice health issues quickly, so they stand out from everyday updates.

Outcome

Shipped

in the teacher and family apps at once.
